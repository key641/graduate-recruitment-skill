# Resume tailoring rules

## Select a base

- AI Product: agents, AI tools, model applications, evaluation, AI infrastructure.
- General Product: consumer, growth, operations, or platform product roles.
- Technical Product: infrastructure, developer tools, data platforms, or technical product roles.
- Design/Research: user research, HCI, interaction, UX, or industrial design.

Use the candidate's configured variants; these categories are guidance, not required filenames.

## Map JD to evidence

For every important JD requirement, record:

1. Exact requirement
2. Strongest verified evidence
3. Metric or artifact, if verified
4. Gap or uncertainty
5. Proposed resume change

Prefer evidence over keyword matching. Do not add domain passion, tools, metrics, or ownership without proof.

## Approval and editing order

1. Present the fit analysis and complete proposed wording in chat.
2. Wait for explicit approval.
3. After approval, duplicate the base resume.
4. Edit title/summary, experience emphasis, first sentence and bullets of the most relevant projects, skills, and project selection.
5. Preserve factual meaning and record a short change log in the tracker.

## Layout rules

- Preserve page count, hierarchy, bullets, alignment, and spacing.
- Shorten text before changing typography.
- Use fonts available to the editing environment.
- If font substitution is required, obtain approval before applying it to the base or tailored copy.
- Visually inspect the final output for wrapping, clipping, missing fonts, and overflow.
