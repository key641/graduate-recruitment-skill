# First-use configuration

Collect only what is required for the requested capabilities.

## Candidate

- Graduation cohort and eligible graduation dates
- Target role families and preferred industries
- Location and work-authorization constraints
- Education, experience, projects, skills, and verified metrics
- Resume facts that must not be inferred

## Sources and destinations

- Job source URLs or connected job database
- Master application tracker and its field mapping
- Recruitment mailbox and search scope
- Calendar destination and event preferences
- Base resume file, base node/page, and available resume variants

## Operating preferences

- Discovery and email-sync cadence
- Definition of valid campus/autumn-recruitment roles
- Fit-ranking priorities
- Whether calendar writing is enabled
- Resume approval rule; default to approval before design-file edits

Do not store credentials, tokens, private links, page IDs, or candidate facts inside the skill package. Keep them in the user's connected apps, workspace configuration, or memory.
